Plot3D for Alibre Design

3D Parametric Formula Studio & Geometric Modeling Engine User Guide

1. Introduction & Architecture Overview

Plot3D for Alibre Design is a mathematical modeling and CAD integration tool designed for engineers, industrial designers, and educators. It enables mathematical synthesis, visualization, and native generation of complex 3D parametric curves, helical paths, mathematical knots, and organic wireframes directly inside Alibre Design Part workspaces (.AD_PR).

Key Architectural Highlights

  • Dual-Mode Execution: Operates as an integrated Alibre Design Add-On (via IAlibreAddOn) or as a standalone parametric formula studio.
  • Compiled Expression Engine: Compiles formula text into direct C# expression tree delegates for near-instant evaluation across thousands of sample points.
  • Curvature-Adaptive Sampling: Dynamically refines sampling density based on local chord error and segment curvature angles, ensuring smooth representation of tight bends without point bloat on linear spans.
  • Native 3D Sketch Generation: Directly constructs cubic B-Splines, segmented Polylines, or discrete Point Nodes inside Alibre's modeling kernel.
  • Automated Unit & Scale Management: Automatically transforms formulas into Alibre's internal Centimeter kernel space while allowing users to work in Millimeters, Inches, Meters, or Feet.

Add-On Registration Procedure

  1. Navigate to the Alibre Design Add-Ons folder (e.g. %APPDATA%\Alibre Design\Add-Ons\).
  2. Place the Plot3D.UI.adaddin manifest into the folder: 
    <?xml version="1.0" encoding="utf-8"?>
<AlibreAddOnManager>
  <AddOn Assembly="Plot3D.UI.dll"
         Class="Plot3D.UI.Alibre.AlibreAddOn"
         Icon="Plot3D.ico"
         MenuText="Plot3D"
         ToolTip="3D Parametric Formula Studio for Alibre Design"
         AutoLoad="1" />
</AlibreAddOnManager>
  3. Launch Alibre Design, open any Part document (.AD_PR), and access the studio via Plot3D > 3D Parametric Formula Studio...

4. Mathematical Formula Engine & Syntax Guide

Formulas in Plot3D define spatial positions (x(t), y(t), z(t)) as continuous functions of the scalar parameter t. The engine supports implicit multiplication, standard constants, and comprehensive elementary and transcendental operations.

5. Adaptive Sampling & Precision Controls

Plot3D utilizes a recursive chord-error and curvature-bounded subdivision algorithm to discretize parametric equations with high CAD fidelity.

Chord Error Tolerance Criterion:
echord = || Pmid − (P0 + P1)/2 || > Tolabs + Tolrel × max(||P0||, ||P1||, ||Pmid||, 1.0)

Curvature Angle Criterion: Subdivides whenever tangent turn angle θ = ∠(Pmid − P0, P1 − Pmid) > 0.15 rad ≈ 8.5°.

6. Alibre Design 3D Sketch Generation

The Alibre integration service maps sampled curve points directly into native geometric entities inside the active Part document.

6.1 Entity Types

  • B-Spline Curve (AddBSplineByPoints) — Recommended: Fits a smooth continuous cubic NURBS curve through all sample points. Best for sweeps, aerodynamic lofts, and smooth organic profiles.
  • Polyline (AddLine): Chains consecutive sample points with linear 3D line segments. Ideal for faceted paths or rapid preview tests.
  • Point Nodes (AddPointNode): Places discrete 3D reference vertices. Ideal for CMM inspection targets or station guides.

6.2 Unit Conversion Matrix

Alibre Design internally defines all part coordinates in Centimeters (AD_CENTIMETERS). Plot3D applies the exact transformation scale factor:

PAlibre (cm) = PFormula × ScaleUser × (ToMm(UnitsInput) / 10.0)
Input Unit Option Conversion Multiplier to Centimeters Resulting Model Scale (Scale = 1.0)
Millimeters (mm) × 0.100 100 mm in formula → 10.0 cm in Alibre
Centimeters (cm) × 1.000 10 cm in formula → 10.0 cm in Alibre
Meters (m) × 100.000 1.0 m in formula → 100.0 cm in Alibre
Inches (in) × 2.540 1.0 in in formula → 2.54 cm in Alibre
Feet (ft) × 30.480 1.0 ft in formula → 30.48 cm in Alibre

7. Downstream CAD Modeling Best Practices

CAD Best Practice: 3D Swept Features (Springs, Tubes, Channels)

1. Insert Normal Plane: Use Feature > Plane with Normal to Curve mode at curve vertex t = tmin.
2. Draw Profile Sketch: Create a 2D circle or custom profile centered coincident with the curve origin.
3. Sweep: Execute Sweep Boss with Profile = 2D Sketch, Path = 3D B-Spline Sketch.
4. Curvature Check: Ensure profile radius rprofile < ρmin to prevent self-intersection.

CAD Best Practice: Guided Surface Lofts

Generate multiple parallel 3D curves in separate sketches to act as guide rails for Loft Boss / Cut across planar cross-section sketches.

10. Troubleshooting & FAQs

Common Issue: "Failed to access Alibre Design active part"

Ensure that an active Part workspace (.AD_PR) is open. Alibre Design 3D sketches cannot be inserted directly into Assembly (.AD_ASM) or Drawing (.AD_DRW) environments.

Common Issue: "B-Spline creation failed; falling back to polyline"

This occurs when an equation has extreme geometric cusps or sharp direction reversals. Plot3D automatically falls back to a high-density polyline to prevent CAD kernel failure.
